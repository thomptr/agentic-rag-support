# Account Takeover Policy

## What Is an Account Takeover?

An account takeover (ATO) occurs when an unauthorized person gains access to your account without your knowledge or consent. Signs of an account takeover include:
- Login notifications for sessions you did not initiate
- Unfamiliar devices in your active sessions list
- Changed account information (email, password, MFA) you did not make
- Unexpected actions taken in your account (data exported, integrations added, settings changed)
- Billing changes or purchases you did not authorize

## Immediate Steps If You Suspect a Takeover

**Act immediately — do not wait:**

1. **Change your password now** — go to Settings → Security → Change Password
2. **Revoke all active sessions** — Settings → Security → Active Sessions → "Sign Out All Devices"
3. **Review and revoke API keys** — Settings → Developer → API Keys
4. **Enable MFA if not already enabled** — Settings → Security → MFA
5. **Contact support** — use the steps below to report the incident

## Reporting a Suspected Account Takeover

**This situation requires escalation to a human security specialist.**

To report:
1. Log in (if you still have access) and go to Help → Report Security Incident
2. Or email security@example.com from your registered email address
3. Or call our security hotline: available in your account's support contact page (Enterprise) or via in-app chat

**Provide the following information**:
- Your account email
- When you first noticed suspicious activity
- What actions appear to have been taken
- Whether your password or MFA has been changed without your authorization

**Response time**: Security incidents are responded to within 2 business hours. Critical incidents (active unauthorized access) are escalated to our 24/7 security team.

## What Happens After You Report

1. **Immediate containment**: We may temporarily lock the account to prevent further unauthorized access
2. **Investigation**: Our security team reviews access logs, IP addresses, device fingerprints, and action history
3. **Restoration**: We work with you to restore your account to its state before the takeover
4. **Report**: We provide a summary of the incident, what was accessed, and steps taken

## How We Handle Sensitive Data During Takeover Incidents

- We will never ask for your password, MFA codes, or security question answers via email or chat
- All verification is done through the official support portal with identity confirmation
- Account data reviewed during the investigation is handled per our Privacy Policy
- If credentials or PII were exposed, we will notify you and may be legally required to report the breach

## Protecting Your Account Going Forward

After a takeover incident:
- Use a unique, strong password (use a password manager)
- Enable TOTP-based MFA (more secure than SMS)
- Audit your connected integrations and revoke any you did not authorize
- Review your billing history for unauthorized charges
- Consider using a dedicated email address for your account

## If You Have Lost Access to Your Account

If the attacker changed your email or password and you cannot log in:
1. Go to the login page and click "Can't access your account?"
2. Follow the account recovery flow (identity verification required)
3. Contact support if the recovery flow is unavailable — we will assist with identity verification using government-issued ID

**Do not file a chargeback** for unauthorized charges before contacting us — we can reverse charges faster through our billing team and a chargeback may complicate the investigation.

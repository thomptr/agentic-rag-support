# Account Deletion

## Overview

Account deletion is a **permanent, irreversible action** that removes your account and all associated data after a 90-day retention window. This is distinct from plan cancellation (which stops billing but preserves your account and data).

## Deletion vs. Cancellation

| Action | Effect | Data |
|--------|--------|------|
| **Cancel subscription** | Billing stops; account enters free/suspended state | Data retained; account accessible |
| **Delete account** | Account permanently removed after 90 days | Data deleted after 90-day window |

If you want to stop paying but keep your data accessible, cancel your subscription rather than deleting your account.

## Permanent Account Deletion Process

Only account **Admins** can initiate account deletion.

1. Export your data first — see **Data Export Procedures** (strongly recommended)
2. Go to **Account Settings → Account → Delete Account**
3. Read the consequences summary carefully
4. Enter your account password to confirm
5. Click **Permanently Delete Account**

You receive an email confirmation. Your account enters the **90-day retention window** immediately.

**You have 30 days to cancel the deletion** by logging back in and clicking **Cancel Deletion** in the notification banner.

## 90-Day Data Retention Window

After deletion is initiated:

- **Days 1–30**: You can cancel the deletion by logging in
- **Days 31–90**: Account is locked (read-only access for data export only); deletion cannot be reversed
- **Day 91+**: All data is permanently deleted from our systems

During the retention window, you can still export your data (see **Data Export Procedures**).

## What Data Is Deleted

The following is permanently deleted:

- All user accounts and profiles associated with the account
- All resources, projects, and workspaces
- All API keys
- All webhook configurations
- All activity and audit logs
- All billing history records (except legally required retention — see below)

## What Data Is Retained for Legal Compliance

We are required by law to retain certain records even after account deletion:

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|
| Invoice and payment records | 7 years | Tax and accounting compliance |
| Fraud and dispute records | 5 years | Legal and compliance requirements |
| Security incident logs | 3 years | Regulatory requirements |

This retained data is not accessible to you but is held securely in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

## Billing Implications

- **Final invoice**: A final invoice is generated at deletion for any charges in the current billing period (prorated if applicable)
- **Outstanding balance**: Any unpaid balance must be resolved before deletion can complete. If you have an outstanding balance, deletion is paused until payment is received.
- **Refunds**: Unused prepaid amounts (annual plans) are refunded pro-rata. Refund processing takes 5–10 business days.
- **No further charges**: Once deletion is confirmed, no further charges are made

## Re-Registration After Deletion

After your account is permanently deleted (after the 90-day window):

- You can re-register with the same email address
- You start with a clean account — no data from the deleted account is restored
- Your previous billing history is not available (though invoices are retained internally for compliance)

If you deleted your account by mistake within the 30-day cancellation window, log in immediately and cancel the deletion.

# Refund Eligibility

## Refund Policy Overview

We offer refunds in specific circumstances. Refund eligibility depends on your plan type, the reason for the refund request, and timing.

## Eligible Refund Scenarios

### 1. Annual Plan — 30-Day Refund Window
- If you subscribed to or renewed an annual plan, you may request a full refund within 30 days of the charge
- Refund amount: Prorated for months remaining after cancellation date
- Condition: Account must be canceled at time of refund request

### 2. Billing Errors
- Duplicate charges (same plan charged twice in the same billing period): Full refund of the duplicate charge
- Incorrect plan charged (e.g., Professional billed instead of Basic): Refund of the price difference
- Charge after cancellation confirmation: Full refund of the erroneous charge

### 3. Service Outages
- If our platform is unavailable for more than 24 cumulative hours in a billing period, you may request a prorated credit or refund for the affected period
- Credit is applied to the next billing cycle; refund can be requested in place of credit

### 4. Unauthorized Charges
- If you did not authorize a charge (e.g., compromised payment method), we will investigate and issue a refund if confirmed fraudulent

## Non-Eligible Scenarios

- Monthly plan cancellation (no prorated refunds for partial months)
- Annual plan cancellation after the 30-day window
- Unused features or functionality
- Dissatisfaction with product features after the 30-day window

## How to Request a Refund

1. Log in and go to Account Settings → Billing → Request Refund
2. Select the invoice and reason for the refund
3. Our team reviews requests within 3 business days
4. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method

## Subscription Status Check

Before processing a refund, our team verifies:
- Current subscription status (active, canceled, suspended)
- Date of the original charge
- Whether the 30-day window applies
- Any prior refunds on the account (limit: one refund per 12-month period per policy)

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds are issued when:
- Upgrading and requesting a refund for the price difference
- Annual plan canceled mid-year within the 30-day window (refund for unused months)
- Service credit converted to cash refund

## Processing Time

- Credit card refunds: 5–10 business days
- ACH/bank transfer refunds: 7–14 business days
- Credits to account balance: Immediate

# Cancellation Terms

## How to Cancel

You can cancel your subscription at any time:
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Account Settings → Billing → Subscription
3. Click "Cancel Subscription"
4. Confirm cancellation in the dialog

Alternatively, contact our billing support team to process the cancellation for you.

## Notice Period

- **Monthly plans**: No notice period required. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
- **Annual plans**: 30 days written notice required before the annual renewal date to avoid being charged for the next year
- Cancellations submitted less than 30 days before annual renewal will be processed for the following year

## What Happens After Cancellation

- Your account remains active until the end of the paid billing period
- Data is retained for 90 days after cancellation in case you want to reactivate
- After 90 days, all data is permanently deleted in accordance with our data retention policy
- You will receive a confirmation email when cancellation is processed

## Prorated Refunds

- **Monthly plans**: No prorated refunds. Service continues until end of billing period
- **Annual plans**: If canceling within the first 30 days after annual renewal, you are eligible for a prorated refund for unused months (see Refund Eligibility policy)
- **Mid-year annual cancellations**: No refunds after the 30-day window; service continues until end of the annual period

## Early Termination Fees

- **Monthly plans**: No early termination fee
- **Annual plans**: No early termination fee if you complete the annual term; cancellations after the 30-day refund window receive no refund but also no additional fee
- **Enterprise contracts**: May include minimum commitment periods; refer to your signed contract for specific terms

## Reactivation

- Reactivate within 90 days by logging in and selecting "Reactivate Subscription"
- Data is restored from the last backup at time of cancellation
- After 90 days, reactivation is possible but data cannot be restored

## Data Export

Before cancellation, we recommend exporting your data:
- Account Settings → Data → Export All Data
- Export includes all tasks, reports, and settings in JSON and CSV formats
- Export processing takes up to 24 hours for large accounts

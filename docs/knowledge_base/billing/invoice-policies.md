# Invoice Policies

## Invoice Generation

Invoices are automatically generated at the start of each billing cycle:
- **Monthly subscribers**: Invoice generated on the same day each month
- **Annual subscribers**: Invoice generated once per year on the renewal date

Invoices are sent to the billing email address on file within 24 hours of charge processing.

## Invoice Contents

Each invoice includes:
- Invoice number and date
- Billing period covered
- Plan name and number of seats/users
- Subtotal, taxes (if applicable), and total charged
- Payment method used (last 4 digits of card)
- Company name and billing address

## Accessing Invoices

Invoices can be accessed in two ways:
1. **Email**: Automatically sent to your billing email after each charge
2. **Dashboard**: Log in → Account Settings → Billing → Invoice History (download PDF)

## Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:
- Credit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- ACH/bank transfer (Enterprise plan only)
- Purchase orders (Enterprise plan only, net-30 terms)

## Payment Timing

- Card payments are processed automatically on the billing date
- ACH transfers require 3–5 business days to process
- Failed payments: We retry automatically after 3 days, then after 7 days. After 3 failed attempts, your account is suspended

## Late Payment

- A 1.5% monthly late fee applies to overdue balances (Enterprise accounts with net-30 terms)
- Accounts with failed payments receive email notifications before suspension
- Grace period: 14 days after the payment due date before service interruption

## Tax Information

- Sales tax is applied based on your billing address location
- If you are tax-exempt, upload your exemption certificate in Account Settings → Billing
- VAT is applied for EU customers; provide your VAT registration number to receive VAT-exclusive invoices

## Duplicate Charges

If you believe you were charged twice:
1. Check your bank statement — some banks show pending and cleared charges separately
2. Review your invoice history in the dashboard
3. If two separate invoices exist for the same period, contact billing support immediately with both invoice numbers

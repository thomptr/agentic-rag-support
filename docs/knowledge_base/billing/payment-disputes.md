# Payment Disputes

## What Is a Payment Dispute?

A payment dispute (also called a chargeback) occurs when you contact your bank or credit card company to reverse a charge on your account. Disputes are distinct from refund requests — they are handled through the card network and involve your financial institution.

## When to Dispute vs. When to Contact Us First

We strongly recommend contacting our billing support team before filing a dispute with your bank:
- Disputes can take 60–120 days to resolve
- A dispute freezes the funds and may trigger account suspension
- We can often resolve billing issues within 1–3 business days

**Contact us first if**:
- You see an unfamiliar charge but have an active account
- You were charged the wrong amount
- You were charged after cancellation

**File a dispute with your bank if**:
- You cannot reach our support team
- Our team has not resolved the issue within 14 days
- The charge is clearly fraudulent (your payment info was stolen)

## Dispute Investigation Process

1. **Receive dispute notice**: We are notified by the card network when you file a dispute
2. **Account review**: We review your account history, invoices, and correspondence (1–3 business days)
3. **Response submission**: We submit evidence to the card network (invoices, usage logs, communication history)
4. **Resolution**: The card network makes a final determination (30–90 days)

## Account Status During a Dispute

- Active accounts with a disputed charge may be suspended pending investigation
- If the dispute is resolved in your favor, the charge is reversed and your account is reinstated
- If resolved in our favor, the original charge stands and the dispute fee ($25) may be applied

## Escalation to Human Agent

**The following dispute scenarios require immediate escalation to a human billing specialist**:
- Disputed charges exceeding $500
- Multiple disputes from the same account
- Suspected fraud or account compromise
- Disputes involving Enterprise contract terms
- Chargebacks involving third-party payment processors (purchase orders, ACH)

If your situation meets any of the above criteria, a human agent will contact you within 2 business hours.

## Preventing Disputes

- Keep your payment method up to date (Account Settings → Billing → Payment Method)
- Review invoices promptly when received
- Set up billing alerts for charges above a threshold
- Ensure your billing email is monitored regularly

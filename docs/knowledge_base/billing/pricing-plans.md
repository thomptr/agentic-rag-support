# Pricing Plans

## Overview

We offer three pricing tiers to meet the needs of individuals, teams, and enterprises.

## Plans

### Basic Plan — $9.99/month
- Up to 5 users
- 10 GB storage
- Standard support (email, 48-hour response)
- Core features: task management, reporting, integrations with 10 apps
- Monthly billing only

### Professional Plan — $29.99/month
- Up to 25 users
- 50 GB storage
- Priority support (email + chat, 8-hour response)
- All Basic features plus: advanced analytics, custom workflows, API access, integrations with 50+ apps
- Monthly or annual billing (annual saves 20%: $287.90/year)

### Enterprise Plan — Custom Pricing
- Unlimited users
- Unlimited storage
- Dedicated support (24/7 phone, email, chat with dedicated account manager)
- All Professional features plus: SSO/SAML, custom SLA, audit logs, on-premise deployment option
- Annual billing required; pricing based on user count and usage

## Billing Cycles

- **Monthly**: Charged on the same date each month (e.g., if you subscribed on the 15th, charged every 15th)
- **Annual**: Charged once per year at the anniversary of your subscription start date
- All prices are in USD and exclusive of applicable taxes

## Plan Changes

- **Upgrades**: Take effect immediately; you are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the billing period
- **Downgrades**: Take effect at the end of the current billing period; no immediate charge adjustment
- You can change your plan at any time from the Account Settings > Billing page

## What Happens If You're Charged More Than Expected

If you see a charge that differs from your plan price:
1. Check if you recently upgraded a plan (prorated charge)
2. Check if your annual renewal processed
3. Check if you added additional users beyond your plan's limit (overage fees apply for Enterprise)
4. Contact billing support with your invoice number for investigation
